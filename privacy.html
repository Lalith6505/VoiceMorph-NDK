Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Absolute Audio Sovereignty: 100% On-Device DSP Execution

VoiceMorph NDK operates with absolute user privacy. All acoustic capture, digital signal processing (DSP), pitch shifting, formant modification, and equalizer filtering are computed exclusively inside your device's native RAM and CPU via Google Oboe C++. At no point is microphone audio, synthesized voice, or processed audio ever streamed, uploaded, cached, or transmitted to any external server or cloud provider.

2. Zero Network Activity & No Internet Permission

VoiceMorph NDK does not declare the Android INTERNET permission in its manifest. The application has zero physical ability to communicate across cellular networks, Wi-Fi, or local network interfaces. It functions continuously in Airplane Mode without restriction.

3. Zero Telemetry, Analytics, or Advertising

The software contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs), no advertising frameworks, no behavioral trackers, and no telemetry or crash-reporting libraries. We collect zero analytics regarding your presets, session durations, or vocal characteristics.

4. Microphone Permission Scope

The RECORD_AUDIO permission is requested exclusively to capture live microphone samples into the local native Oboe input buffer for real-time DSP transformation and loopback monitoring. Samples are discarded in real time unless explicitly recorded to the internal vault by user action.

5. Local Recording Vault & Data Retention

Audio recordings made within the app are stored exclusively in your device's private application sandbox. Recordings remain completely under your control and can be permanently deleted at any time through the in-app vault interface.

6. Policy Contact

For questions or technical inquiries regarding VoiceMorph NDK's privacy design, please inspect the open documentation repository or contact the development team.
